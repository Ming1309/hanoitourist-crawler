# Domestic Tour Catalogue

Language for captured tour details and their acceptance into the domestic catalogue.

## Language

**Detail attempt**:
A recorded effort to capture and interpret the details of one catalogue tour. An unsuccessful attempt does not replace an accepted snapshot.

**Accepted snapshot**:
Complete tour details accepted for a catalogue identity. Later incomplete or rejected attempts do not displace it.
_Avoid_: Latest snapshot (which may not be accepted)

**Attempt completion**:
The determination of a detail attempt's outcome, including whether its evidence changes the catalogue's accepted snapshot or availability. Recovery completion uses already committed evidence rather than acquiring or interpreting new evidence.

**Scoped retry**:
A manual attempt restricted to explicitly selected identities from a frozen batch's unsuccessful detail attempts. It does not establish catalogue discovery coverage.

**Frozen batch**:
A batch whose results and historical display references have been fixed for subsequent reporting and export.
